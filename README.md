Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene"** ($2 reward pool).

## The entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) scores three candidate family scenes
against the judge's five published criteria — real warm family scene,
heartfelt affection, light recognisable humour, tight one-or-two-line use,
specific and fresh — and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`.

The winning scene (161 characters, one line):

> My little brother still saves the last bite of his birthday cake for Dad, who is allergic to cake — and Dad still eats it, every year, smiling through the hives.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
# writes /output/entry.txt (OUTPUT_PATH overrides the path for local testing)
```

Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output. No network, no secrets, no environment variables.
